# Tooling options and adopted services

Keep project-specific conclusions here. Consult the global Tooling Alternatives Library when available and do not duplicate its entire catalog.

Every material tooling decision compares paid/managed, free-tier, open-source/self-hosted, build-it-ourselves, and defer/no-tool lanes. Revalidate pricing, licensing, and product status from primary sources and record the verification date, cost model, platforms and mobile usability, privacy, maintenance, maturity, portability, lock-in, recommendation, and revisit triggers.

The current cross-discipline comparison and adopted stack are recorded in `docs/production/TOOLCHAIN_STRATEGY.md`. The default selection rule is: keep an editable professional source of truth, use AI for bounded acceleration, buy a subscription only for a defined production batch, and avoid a new engine, API, or management platform unless measured project friction justifies its migration and maintenance cost.
