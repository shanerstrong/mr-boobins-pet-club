## Findings

### JACK3D-P1-001 — High — likeness/head anatomy

Front and three-quarter views use a round cranium, spherical cheek pads, flat eye slabs, a pink oval nose with pasted-on details, and a separate mouth/tongue/fang construction. Side view ends the muzzle in an orb. Photos 3-5 and the approved sheet require a continuous shepherd forehead-to-muzzle wedge, embedded almond eyes and upper lids, a broad organic nose with integrated nostrils/philtrum, and a side-opening smile.

Recommendation: rebuild the head as connected anatomy; hold muzzle projection near 48% of head depth and nose width near 42% of muzzle/cheek width; integrate sockets/lids, nose alar form, philtrum, lower jaw, mouth corners, rooted tongue, and restrained side teeth.

### JACK3D-P1-002 — High — body/anatomy/silhouette

The torso reads as a pear-shaped egg/barrel over equal pipe legs and inflated detached feet. The approved direction requires a shoulder-to-deep-chest transition, a 10-15% narrower waist, a readable pelvis, tapered limb joints, planted soles, and sturdy paws near 115% neutral baby scale.

Recommendation: rebuild the torso and limbs as tapered connected cross sections; verify all soles at Z=0 and preserve the approximately 2.7-head-length standing proportion.

### JACK3D-P1-003 — High — topology effectiveness

The triangle count is numerically acceptable but allocated poorly. Spherical smoothing consumes 92.5% of triangles while the muzzle, outer ears, joints, and tail remain extremely coarse and show primitive seams.

Recommendation: reclaim 3k-5k triangles from bulk spheres and spend them on welded head/muzzle transitions, eyelids, curved ear bowls, shoulder/limb joints, paws, and a continuous tapered tail. Require no visible primitive seam in the four review views.

### JACK3D-P1-004 — Medium — material/render quality

The failure is not pixel starvation. Fur values merge into one another and the floor; eyes/nose lack depth; limb, paw, jaw, ear, and tail boundaries collapse at reduced viewing size.

Recommendation: after geometry repair, render final review evidence at 2048 x 2048 with at least 64 CPU samples and denoising; separate warm fur-light and cool fur-shade planes and give eyes/nose a distinct surface response.

### JACK3D-P1-005 — Medium — status correctness

`production-candidate` is unsupported while high findings remain.

Recommendation: use `hero-refinement-required` until a later pass clears all high findings.
